The Cuban Sandwich

The Cuban Sandwich was created as a cheap way to fuel workers during the missile crisis in 1962

It takes roughly 2-3 hours if making from scratch, but if shredded pork/steak is already available, then it only takes 15 or so minutes

Tips on preparation

The best way to prepare the meat is to slow-roast the pork or if using steak, baste it to bring out more of the flavour

First we eat, then we do everything else

M.F.K Fisher

Ingredients

Sirloin steak, Gruyere cheese, Focaccia bread, Mustard, Pickles, Relish, Steak sauce (optional)

Preparing the filling

    - Place a Sirloin Steak in a pan and drizzle some oil, butter and put any herb on it (e.g. thyme)
    - Set the heat to 160 degrees (gas level 5 if using gas stove, level 4 if using electric induction stove) and let it cook for 30 mins
    - Sear both sides evenly and with the butter melted now, baste the steak while searing it to get an even coat of flavour
    - Once done, cut a loaf of focaccia, layer the slice with sauce of choice (the best one to go with the overall taste would be mustard), then slice the steak at a 30 degree/diagonally to maximise the taste when eating and place evenly on the bottom half
    - Cut a pickle/pickles into thin slices then lay them on top of the steak
    - Place a few slices of Gruyere cheese and melt it with a flame-torch
    - (OPTIONAL) Layer some relish on top to bring out the pickles' flabour profile
    Enjoy.

To maximise enjoyment...

Pair this up with a side of thick-cut oven chips and a pint of Stella Artois in the evening, or to make it fancier, drink with a glass of 1947 Chateau Mouignon
